| cell class | result | S cost |

rustsrc L19: 1.00 -> 1.45). This is a much heavier product regression
than brotli's fragment band (which still compresses 14-25%); it is
NOT shipped because it does not clear the bar alone and its ratio
cost needs explicit owner selection.

## The structurally-beyond cells

The zstd **L1 column** (words 3.65, rustsrc 2.64, fits 2.4, csv2m
1.98, plists 1.5) has no tier below it — L1 IS the bottom compressed
tier; our implementation is ZSTD_fast's faithful shape (task 43) and
its per-position loop is the six-times-confirmed floor. Clearing
these five cells requires either (a) raw/RLE-block output — i.e.
shipping NON-COMPRESSION — which I decline without the owner naming
it explicitly, or (b) breaking the workspace's #![forbid(unsafe_code)]
invariant (unchecked loads), which is a codebase-values decision,
not an engineering one.

## Position

1.2 is achievable for 18/23 cells only via the maximal trade above,
with 5 cells (the L1 column) beyond every honest lever. The complete
choice set: (i) accept the 23-cell floor as terminal; (ii) authorize
the down-tier band (partial clear, heavy ratio cost); (iii) authorize
raw-block L1 (full clear, ships non-compression); (iv) lift the
unsafe-code invariant for the five hot loops (full clear, changes the
codebase's core guarantee). Each is recorded here with its measured
consequence; none is taken unilaterally.
