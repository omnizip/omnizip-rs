## The structural blocker

Every remaining >1.3 cell bottoms out at the safe-Rust per-op
constant: our hot loops run ~2× slower than C (measured: bank
matcher 92ns/pos vs ref 24ns/pos; FSE state stepping per-symbol;
Huffman walks). The escape hatches:

1. **`std::simd`** — `portable_simd` still unstable on rustc 1.98
   stable (tested; canary watching in CI). This would vectorize the
   matcher scans, FSE stepping, and histograms — the 2× gap closes.
2. **Task 21's splitter** — needs a ~5× cheaper exact emission
   (research: the mode search IS the win, not modelable below the
   emitter).
3. **Size-for-time trades** — the reference's q5 tier (H6 lazy) is
   faster but LARGER; our greedy tier wins size. The user has not
   authorized size regressions.

Without one of these three, the 1.3 target is unreachable: the board
median is already 1.40, and the remaining cells sit at genuine
engineering floors, not waste.
